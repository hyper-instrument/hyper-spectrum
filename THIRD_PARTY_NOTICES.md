## Denoising Framework for X-ray Absorption Spectroscopy Data

- Title: *Denoising Framework for X-ray Absorption Spectroscopy Data*
- Authors: Tomas Aidukas, Ilnura Usmanova, Benjamín Béjar Haro, Maarten
  Nachtegaal, and Adam H. Clark
- Record and DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17434349>
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0),
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- HyperSpectrum use and changes: HyperSpectrum references the record's
  nonuniform XASDenoise checkpoint filename, size, and digest, and documents
  the preprocessing contract needed for compatible inference. The checkpoint
  is not redistributed or downloaded by HyperSpectrum. HyperSpectrum's adapter,
  validation, provenance, and fail-closed execution behavior are new code; the
  Zenodo asset itself is not modified.

## In-situ XANES data for calcined doped LaPO4 monazite

- Title: *In-situ XANES data for the calcination of Ca(II)/Ce(IV)-doped LaPO4
  monazite*
- Creator: Theresa Lender; contributors Nina Huittinen and Elena Bazarkina
- Record and DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10606662>
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0),
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- HyperSpectrum use and changes: the deterministic XANES materializer targets
  the record's SPEC layout. `tests/fixtures/xas/spec-real-header.txt` is a
  three-line excerpt containing only the scan, column-count, and column-name
  headers; measurement rows are not redistributed. The excerpt was separated
  from the source archive and stored as a compact parser fixture. Synthetic
  values used by tests are generated by HyperSpectrum and are not record data.
